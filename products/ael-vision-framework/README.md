# AEL Vision Framework

**AEL Digital Studio** — Core methodology: Design defines — AI delivers.

---

## Overview

The AEL Vision Framework (v2.2) is the operational methodology that governs all AEL projects. It establishes the principle that design drives the vision while AI powers the build — the "what" precedes the "how."

## Status

**Active** — v2.2

## Brand Integration

This product is the philosophical backbone of the AEL brand. The Three Pillars (Artist Over Tool, Substance Over Noise, Engine Over Output) are expressions of the Vision Framework.
